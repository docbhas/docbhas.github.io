## What it does
A desktop-web simulator for normal adult transthoracic echocardiography in the POCUS style:
- **Learn:** guided lessons with 3D surface anatomy.
- **Scan:** move a virtual probe over the torso, with an optional phone as a motion-sensing probe.
- **Analysis:** 2D measurement, Doppler and M-mode, basic hemodynamics.
