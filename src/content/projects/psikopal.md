## What it does
Four scoring tools on one offline page:
1. **SSPT** (Shatri Sinulingga Psychosomatic Test): positive when the score is 2 or more.
2. **Adult palliative screening:** underlying disease, comorbidity, ECOG, and other criteria; intervention when the score is 3 or more.
3. **HADS:** anxiety and depression (0–7 normal, 8–10 borderline, 11 or more abnormal), answered by the patient or caregiver.
4. **PPS and PPI:** group A (4 or less), B (4.5–6), C (more than 6).
