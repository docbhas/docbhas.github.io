## What it does
- **On-call mode:** a quick fitness-for-surgery screen for emergency or urgent cases (about 3–5 minutes): CFS, RCRI, ARISCAT, Caprini, IMPROVE, Morse.
- **Outpatient mode:** a comprehensive assessment before elective or time-sensitive surgery: FRAIL and CFS, MMSE, MNA-SF, ACC/AHA 2024, PHQ-2, and advance care planning.
- Source document viewable inside the app, read only.
